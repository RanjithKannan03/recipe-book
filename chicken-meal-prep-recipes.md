# Part 1: Homemade Seasonings

## How to use the blends

Each blend makes **one week's worth** (about 5 tbsp), enough for:
- **600g diced chicken breast**: about 2 tbsp blend + 1 tbsp oil
- **900g chicken thigh fillets**: about 3 tbsp blend + 1½ tbsp oil

**Method (all blends):**
1. Put the chicken in a bowl and pour over the oil.
2. Add the blend plus the "wet" extra listed for that flavour.
3. Mix well and leave 15 minutes. Overnight in the fridge is even better.
4. Air fry, shaking the basket halfway:
   - **Diced breast:** 200°C for about 12–15 minutes
   - **Thigh fillets:** 200°C for about 18–22 minutes
   - Chicken is done at **75°C** in the thickest part (no pink, juices run clear).

**Tips**
- Multiply a blend by 4 and keep it in a jar for a month. Shake before use, because salt sinks.
- Blends with sugar (BBQ, jerk) can burn, so cook those at 180°C for a few minutes longer.
- First batch tastes off? Next time adjust: more salt if flat, more chilli for heat, more lemon/lime for tang.
- Keep jars in a cool, dark cupboard. Blends lose flavour after about 6 months.
- Cooked blend chicken: cool, refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below and eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days), or freeze portions for up to 3 months and thaw overnight in the fridge. Reheat in the air fryer at 180°C for 4–5 minutes until piping hot.
- Freeze it raw: bag the chicken with the oil, blend and wet extras, flatten and freeze. It marinates as it thaws overnight in the fridge.
- Buy spices in big bags from Aldi, Lidl or the world foods aisle. Most blends share a paprika, garlic, onion, salt and pepper base.

## Peri-peri
*Replaces the Bramwells peri-peri packets.*

- 1½ tbsp paprika (plain paprika is sweet paprika)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½–1½ tsp chilli powder or cayenne (½ = mild, 1½ = hot)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Wet:** oil + zest and juice of 1 lemon per 600g chicken + 1 tbsp vinegar if you have it (any kind; plain white works, malt is stronger so use 2 tsp). Keep a wedge back and squeeze it over after cooking; the heat dulls the lemon, so the fresh squeeze is what makes it tangy.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## Smoky all-rounder

- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic granules
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano or mixed herbs
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½–1 tsp cayenne or chilli flakes
- 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)

**Wet:** just oil.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## Fajita

- 1 tbsp mild chilli powder
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Wet:** oil, plus a squeeze of lime after cooking. Great on diced breast.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## BBQ

- 1½ tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp chilli powder

**Wet:** oil + a splash of Worcestershire sauce. Cook at 180°C.

**Air fryer:** 180°C · diced breast 15–18 min · thighs 22–25 min

## Cajun

- 1½ tbsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Wet:** just oil.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## Jerk

- 2 tsp ground allspice
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- 1–2 tsp cayenne
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Wet:** oil + juice of ½ lime per 600g chicken + a splash of soy sauce. Best marinated overnight. Cook at 180°C.

**Air fryer:** 180°C · diced breast 15–18 min · thighs 22–25 min

## Tandoori

- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 2 tsp salt

**Wet:** a few spoons of plain yoghurt + juice of ½ lemon per 600g chicken, with only a little oil. Excellent on thighs.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## Lemon & herb

- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp dried parsley
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Wet:** oil + zest and juice of 1 lemon per 600g chicken.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

## Chinese salt & pepper

- 2 tsp Chinese five spice
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar

**Wet:** oil + a splash of soy sauce (that's why the salt is lower). Add sliced onions and peppers for the last 5 minutes.

**Air fryer:** 200°C · diced breast 12–15 min · thighs 18–22 min

# Part 2: Recipes

## 1. Hot honey chicken tenders (pickle brine version)
*Sweet, spicy and crispy, served with ranch.*

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · butter ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 12–15 min · turn and spray with oil halfway

### Chicken
- 900g (2 lb) chicken breast, cut into strips (or tenderloins)
- 2 cups buttermilk
- ⅓ cup pickle juice
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Cooking oil spray

### Flour mixture
- 2 cups plain flour
- ½ cup cornflour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp black pepper

### Hot honey sauce
- ¼ cup butter
- 2–3 tbsp hot sauce (e.g. Frank's RedHot)
- ⅓ cup honey

### Method
1. Cut the chicken into strips about 2.5cm thick and 8–10cm long.
2. Mix the chicken with the buttermilk, pickle juice and chicken seasonings. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight for more tender chicken.
3. In a large bowl, mix the flour, cornflour, baking powder and flour seasonings.
4. Drizzle a little marinade into the flour and stir to make small crumbly bits; these give extra crunch.
5. Shake excess marinade off each strip, coat well in flour and shake off the extra. Rest on a rack for 10 minutes.
6. Spray all over with oil, covering any dry flour patches. Air fry in a single layer at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, turning and spraying again halfway, until golden and 75°C inside. Cook in batches if needed.
7. Melt the butter over medium-low heat, then whisk in the honey and hot sauce until smooth and warm.
8. Toss or brush the tenders with the sauce and serve straight away with ranch.

### Healthier
- **The hot honey sauce is most of the sugar and fat.** Make it with half the honey and half the butter, and add an extra splash of hot sauce for kick. That saves about 85 kcal and 11g of free sugars per portion.
- Brush the sauce on thinly, or serve it on the side for dipping, so you use less.
- Serve with salad or veg instead of chips.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Brine overnight for the most tender chicken, but no longer than 24 hours or the texture goes soft.
- Use one hand for wet and one for dry when coating, so your fingers don't clump with batter.
- Spray every floury patch: dry flour stays white and powdery in the air fryer.
- Leave a gap between tenders so the hot air reaches every side.

### Storage
- Store the tenders plain. Keep the hot honey sauce in a jar in the fridge for up to a week and warm it gently before tossing.
- **Fridge:** cool on a rack so the bottoms don't steam, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in a box lined with kitchen paper. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked plain tenders on a tray, then bag them; they keep for 3 months. Or freeze the coated raw tenders the same way and air fry from frozen at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, turning and spraying halfway, until 75°C inside.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 5–6 minutes from the fridge or 10–12 minutes from frozen, until piping hot. Avoid the microwave; it makes the coating soggy.
- Keep ranch in the fridge and use within 3 days; don't freeze it.

## 2. Hot honey chicken tenders (garlic ranch version)

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · unsalted butter ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 12–15 min · turn and spray with oil halfway

### Chicken
- 680g (1½ lb) chicken tenders, or breast cut into strips

### Marinade
- 1¼ cups buttermilk
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne (optional)

### Crispy coating
- 2 cups plain flour
- ½ cup cornflour
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp baking powder
- Cooking oil spray

### Hot honey glaze
- 3 tbsp honey
- 4 tbsp Frank's Buffalo sauce
- 1½ tbsp unsalted butter
- Squeeze of lemon juice

### Garlic ranch dip (optional)
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp full-fat Greek yoghurt (or soured cream)
- 1 tsp grated garlic
- ½ tsp dried dill
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Garnish
- Fresh parsley

### Method
1. Mix the marinade, add the chicken and marinate at least 1 hour (overnight is best).
2. Mix the coating ingredients. Rub 2–3 tbsp of marinade into the flour with your fingers to form small crumbles.
3. Coat each tender wet → dry → wet → dry, pressing the flour on firmly. Rest 10 minutes.
4. Spray all over with oil, covering any dry flour patches. Air fry in a single layer at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, turning and spraying again halfway, until golden and 75°C inside. Cook in batches if needed. Rest on a wire rack 1–2 minutes.
5. Warm the glaze ingredients over low heat until smooth and glossy; don't let it boil.
6. Whisk the dip ingredients together and chill until needed.
7. Brush the warm glaze over the chicken just before serving. Garnish with parsley.

### Healthier
- **Halve the honey and butter in the glaze** and add an extra splash of Buffalo sauce. That saves about 50 kcal and 8g of free sugars per portion.
- The garlic ranch dip isn't counted on the nutrition card because it's optional. If you have it, make it with light mayo and 0% Greek yoghurt.
- Serve with salad or veg instead of chips.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Overnight in the buttermilk gives the juiciest tenders.
- The marinade crumbles rubbed into the flour (step 2) are what make the coating craggy; don't skip them.
- Press the final flour layer on firmly and let it rest the full 10 minutes so it sticks.
- Keep the glaze below a simmer; boiling makes the butter split.

### Storage
- Store the tenders plain. Keep the glaze in a jar in the fridge for up to a week and warm it gently before brushing.
- **Fridge:** cool on a rack so the bottoms don't steam, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in a box lined with kitchen paper. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked plain tenders on a tray, then bag them; they keep for 3 months. Or freeze the coated raw tenders the same way and air fry from frozen at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, turning and spraying halfway, until 75°C inside.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 5–6 minutes from the fridge or 10–12 minutes from frozen, until piping hot. Avoid the microwave; it makes the coating soggy.
- Keep the garlic ranch dip in the fridge and use within 3 days; don't freeze it.

## 3. Honey sriracha garlic chicken tenders

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · butter ×0 · mayonnaise → light mayo · soured cream → 0% fat Greek yoghurt

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 12–15 min · turn and spray with oil halfway

### Chicken
- 450g (1 lb) chicken breast or tenders, cut into small strips
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp white pepper

### Buttermilk batter
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp hot sauce

### Dry coating
- 1 cup plain flour
- ½ cup cornflour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Salt, to taste
- ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)
- Cooking oil spray

### Honey sriracha garlic glaze
- ⅓ cup honey
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp fresh garlic, finely minced
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp chilli flakes (optional)

### Ranch dressing
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup soured cream
- 2–3 tbsp buttermilk
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried dill
- ¼ tsp dried parsley
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Salt, to taste

### Method
1. Toss the chicken with all the chicken seasonings and leave 15–20 minutes.
2. Whisk the buttermilk, egg and hot sauce in one bowl. Mix the dry coating ingredients in another.
3. Dip chicken in buttermilk, coat in flour, dip again, coat again. Press the final layer on firmly for a craggy finish. Rest on a rack 10 minutes.
4. Spray all over with oil, covering any dry flour patches. Air fry in a single layer at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, turning and spraying again halfway, until golden and 75°C inside. Cook in batches if needed.
5. Simmer all the glaze ingredients over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring, until smooth and glossy.
6. Whisk the ranch ingredients together.
7. Toss the chicken in the glaze just before serving.

### Healthier
- **The ranch dressing is the biggest saving.** Use light mayo instead of mayonnaise and 0% Greek yoghurt instead of soured cream; it stays creamy.
- **Halve the honey and skip the butter in the glaze.** The sriracha, soy and garlic still carry the flavour.
- Together that's about 350 kcal less and 19g less free sugar per portion.
- Serve the ranch on the side for dipping rather than drizzling it over.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Cut the strips evenly so they all finish cooking at the same time.
- Double dipping (step 3) gives the thickest crust; press the last layer on firmly.
- The glaze thickens as it cools. Loosen it with a splash of water before tossing.
- Toss just before eating, not before storing.

### Storage
- Store the tenders plain. Keep the glaze in a jar in the fridge for up to a week.
- **Fridge:** cool on a rack so the bottoms don't steam, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in a box lined with kitchen paper. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked plain tenders on a tray, then bag them; they keep for 3 months. Or freeze the coated raw tenders the same way and air fry from frozen at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, turning and spraying halfway, until 75°C inside.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 5–6 minutes from the fridge or 10–12 minutes from frozen, until piping hot. Avoid the microwave; it makes the coating soggy.
- Keep ranch in the fridge and use within 3 days; don't freeze it.

## 4. Bang bang chicken tenders (air fryer)
*High protein, quick and crunchy.*

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · sweet chilli sauce ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 15 min

### Chicken
- 300g chicken tenders (about 6)
- 1 egg
- 30g flour
- 50g cornflakes, crushed
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- Salt and pepper
- 10 sprays cooking oil

### Bang bang sauce
- 100g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 30g sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 20g honey
- Extra 5g sweet chilli sauce per tender

### Method
1. Season the tenders with salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder.
2. Set up three bowls: flour, whisked egg, crushed cornflakes.
3. Coat each tender in flour, then egg, then cornflakes.
4. Put in the air fryer and spray with oil.
5. Air fry at 200°C for 15 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, mix the sauce ingredients.
7. Spread a little extra sweet chilli on each tender, then dip in the bang bang sauce.

**Per 3 tenders:** 477 kcal · 50g protein · 58g carbs · 5g fat

### Healthier
- This one is already light: cornflake crumb and a 0% Greek yoghurt sauce.
- **Halve the honey and sweet chilli in the bang bang sauce** and add an extra drop of sriracha. That cuts free sugars from about 24g to 14g per portion.
- Skip the extra sweet chilli drizzle per tender.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Crush the cornflakes to a mix of fine crumbs and pea-sized pieces: the crumbs stick and the pieces crunch.
- Press the cornflakes on firmly with your palm.
- Turn the tenders halfway for even colour, and check at 12 minutes, since cornflakes brown faster than flour.

### Storage
- Store the tenders plain; add the sweet chilli and sauce only when eating.
- **Fridge:** cool on a rack so the bottoms don't steam, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in a box lined with kitchen paper. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked plain tenders on a tray, then bag them; they keep for 3 months. Or freeze the coated raw tenders the same way and air fry from frozen at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, turning and spraying halfway, until 75°C inside.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 5–6 minutes from the fridge or 10–12 minutes from frozen, until piping hot. Avoid the microwave; it makes the coating soggy.
- The bang bang sauce is yoghurt-based: keep it in the fridge for 2–3 days and don't freeze it. It takes 2 minutes to make fresh.

## 5. Chicken shawarma with pomegranate glaze

**Lighter:** oil ×½ · garlic dip ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · chicken 18–20 min, wraps 3–4 min · shake halfway

### Chicken
- 900g (2 lb) boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp garlic dip (e.g. Toom)
- 1 tsp red pepper paste
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tbsp oil for marinating

### Shawarma glaze
- ½ cup oil
- 1 tbsp red pepper paste or tomato purée
- 1 tbsp sumac
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses

### Sumac onions
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- Squeeze of lemon juice
- 1 tsp sumac
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp oil

### To assemble
- 6 thin pitta breads
- 1 cup tomato, deseeded and diced
- Chips
- About 225g garlic dip

### Method
1. Mix the chicken with all the dry spices, then add the lemon juice, garlic dip, red pepper paste, tomato purée and oil. Coat well, cover, and marinate at least 2 hours or overnight.
2. Mix the glaze ingredients in a small bowl.
3. Toss the sumac onion ingredients together.
4. Air fry the chicken at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, shaking halfway, until cooked through and slightly charred. Cook in batches if the basket is full.
5. Spread garlic sauce on a pitta, add chicken, chips, sumac onions and tomatoes. Roll tightly.
6. Brush the wrap with the glaze and air fry at 200°C for 3–4 minutes until golden and crispy.
7. Serve warm with extra garlic sauce and chips.

### Healthier
- **The glaze oil and garlic dip are most of the calories.** Use half the oil in the marinade, glaze and onions, and half the garlic dip. That's about 345 kcal less per portion.
- Brush the glaze on thinly; it only needs to coat.
- Swap some of the garlic dip for 0% Greek yoghurt with grated garlic and lemon if you want more sauce.
- Leave out the chips and add extra salad to the pitta.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Thighs stay juicier than breast and reheat better, so they're the best choice for meal prep.
- Marinate overnight for the deepest flavour.
- Let the edges char a little in the air fryer; that's the shawarma flavour.

### Rolling
- Warm each pitta for 30 seconds in the air fryer at 180°C first. Cold bread cracks when rolled.
- Spread the garlic sauce over the whole pitta, right to the edges, so it glues the wrap shut.
- Lay the filling in a line across the lower third, leaving about 3cm clear at each side. Don't overfill: a small handful of chicken is plenty.
- Keep wet fillings like tomato in the middle, away from the edges.
- Fold the bottom edge up and tuck it tightly under the filling, fold in one side to close the end, then roll away from you, keeping it tight.
- Put it in the air fryer seam-side down so the seam seals as it crisps.
- Wrap the bottom half in foil or baking paper to hold it while you eat.

### Storage
- **Fridge:** cool, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in an airtight box. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked chicken in single portions for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and eat within 24 hours; don't refreeze.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 4–5 minutes until piping hot, with a splash of water or a little extra oil if it looks dry. Reheat only once.
- **Freeze it raw:** bag the raw chicken in its marinade, flatten and freeze. It finishes marinating as it thaws overnight in the fridge; then just air fry.
- Keep the glaze in a jar in the fridge for up to a week; brush it on after reheating so it doesn't burn.
- Sumac onions are best the same day and keep up to 2 days in the fridge.
- **Don't store assembled wraps:** the bread goes soggy. Pack the chicken, onions and salad separately and roll on the day.

## 6. Chicken shawarma wrap

**Lighter:** ghee ×½ · mayonnaise → light mayo · soured cream → 0% fat Greek yoghurt

**Air fryer:** 200°C · sliced breast 12–15 min, thighs 15–18 min · shake halfway

### Chicken
- 900g (2 lb) boneless chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp fresh garlic, finely minced
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 tbsp ghee or oil, melted, tossed through before air frying

### Sumac onions
- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced and rinsed
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tbsp sumac
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt, to taste

### Garlic sauce
- ⅓ cup mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup soured cream
- 3–4 tbsp buttermilk (adjust for thickness)
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp hot sauce
- 1 tsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- Salt, to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper

### To assemble
- 6 tortilla wraps
- ½ cup cucumber, chopped
- ½ cup tomatoes, chopped
- Spiced chips
- ½ cup pickled cucumbers (gherkins)

### Method
1. Mix the chicken with the spices, salt, mayonnaise, garlic, oil and lemon juice. Cover and marinate at least 1 hour.
2. Whisk all the garlic sauce ingredients until smooth and creamy.
3. Toss the sumac onion ingredients together.
4. Toss the chicken with the melted ghee and air fry at 200°C for 12–15 minutes (sliced breast) or 15–18 minutes (thighs), shaking halfway, until cooked through. Cook in batches so the basket isn't crowded.
5. Warm a tortilla, then add sauce, sumac onions, chicken, tomatoes, cucumber, gherkins, chips and more sauce. Wrap and serve.

### Healthier
- **Halve the ghee** tossed through before air frying; the chicken still chars.
- **Garlic sauce:** use light mayo instead of mayonnaise and 0% Greek yoghurt instead of soured cream.
- Together that's about 155 kcal less per portion.
- Leave out the spiced chips and add extra cucumber and tomato.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Slice the chicken thinly across the grain; thin slices cook fast and stay tender.
- Toss in the melted ghee right before air frying for crisp, charred edges.
- Rinsing the onion takes the harsh bite out.

### Rolling
- Warm each tortilla for 30 seconds in the air fryer at 180°C so it bends without cracking.
- Spread sauce over the middle, then pile the filling in a rectangle just below the centre, leaving about 5cm clear at each side.
- Fold the bottom edge over the filling and pull it back snugly, fold in both sides, then roll up tightly.
- For a crisp, sealed wrap, air fry seam-side down at 200°C for 2–3 minutes.
- Cut in half on a slant and wrap the bottom in foil to hold it together.

### Storage
- **Fridge:** cool, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in an airtight box. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked chicken in single portions for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and eat within 24 hours; don't refreeze.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 4–5 minutes until piping hot, with a splash of water or a little extra oil if it looks dry. Reheat only once.
- **Freeze it raw:** bag the raw chicken in its marinade, flatten and freeze. It finishes marinating as it thaws overnight in the fridge; then just air fry.
- Keep the garlic sauce in the fridge and use within 3 days; don't freeze it.
- **Don't store assembled wraps:** the bread goes soggy. Pack the chicken, onions and salad separately and roll on the day.

## 7. Crispy chicken lavash wrap

**Lighter:** mayonnaise → light mayo · soured cream → 0% fat Greek yoghurt · chips ×0

**Air fryer:** 200°C · chicken 15–18 min, wraps 3–4 min · shake halfway

### Chicken
- 900g (2 lb) boneless chicken breast and thighs, bite-sized pieces
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red pepper paste (or 1 tbsp tomato purée + 1 tsp chilli flakes)
- 3 garlic cloves, finely minced

### Garlic sauce
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup soured cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2–3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Salt, to taste

### To assemble
- 4 large lavash breads
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ cup tomatoes, deseeded and diced
- ½ cup pickled cucumbers, sliced
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 cup chips
- Chopped parsley (optional)

### Method
1. Marinate the chicken with all the seasonings, lemon juice, olive oil, red pepper paste and garlic for at least 1 hour or overnight.
2. Air fry at 200°C for 15–18 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
3. Whisk the garlic sauce ingredients until smooth.
4. Spread garlic sauce over each lavash, then layer lettuce, chicken, tomatoes, onion, pickles and chips. Drizzle with more sauce and roll tightly.
5. Brush lightly with oil and air fry seam-side down at 200°C for 3–4 minutes until golden and crispy.
6. Slice in half and serve.

### Healthier
- **Leave the chips out of the wrap**; add extra lettuce, tomato and cucumber instead.
- **Garlic sauce:** use light mayo instead of mayonnaise and 0% Greek yoghurt instead of soured cream.
- Together that's about 155 kcal less per portion.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- A mix of breast and thighs gives lean bites with juicy ones; all thighs reheat best.
- Keep the pieces the same size so they cook evenly.

### Rolling
- Lavash dries out and cracks quickly. Keep the breads under a clean tea towel, and if one feels stiff, sprinkle it with a little water and warm it for 20 seconds in the air fryer.
- Spread the garlic sauce right to the edges; it holds the roll together.
- Layer the filling in a strip along one short end, leaving about 5cm clear at the long sides.
- Fold both long sides in over the filling, then roll tightly from the filled end, tucking as you go.
- Air fry seam-side down (step 5) so the seam seals.
- Slice in half on a slant with a sharp knife.

### Storage
- **Fridge:** cool, then refrigerate within 2 hours at 5°C or below in an airtight box. Eat within 3–4 days (UK guidance is 2 days, so freeze anything for later in the week if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze cooked chicken in single portions for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and eat within 24 hours; don't refreeze.
- **Reheat:** air fry at 180°C for 4–5 minutes until piping hot, with a splash of water or a little extra oil if it looks dry. Reheat only once.
- **Freeze it raw:** bag the raw chicken in its marinade, flatten and freeze. It finishes marinating as it thaws overnight in the fridge; then just air fry.
- Keep the garlic sauce in the fridge and use within 3 days; don't freeze it.
- Keep the lettuce dry in a box lined with kitchen paper so it stays crisp.
- **Don't store assembled wraps:** the bread goes soggy. Pack the chicken, onions and salad separately and roll on the day.

## 8. Honey butter chicken sandwich
*Seasoned thighs, honey butter, provolone and quick pickled red onions. Prep a week of lunches, toast on the day.*

**Makes:** 4 sandwiches

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · provolone ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · whole thighs 12–18 min, strips 10–12 min · sandwiches 180°C 6–8 min

### Chicken
- 900g boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 8), 2 per sandwich
- 1 tbsp oil

### Spice mix
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder

### Honey butter
- 6 tbsp light spreadable butter, softened
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (or 1 tsp dried, optional)

### Pickled red onions
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar (or white wine vinegar or lemon juice)
- 1 tsp honey
- Pinch of salt

### To assemble
- 8 slices sourdough or panini bread
- 8 slices thin-cut provolone
- Spinach or rocket (optional)
- Frozen mixed veg, to serve on the side

### Method
1. **Onions first, on the clean plate.** Cut the top off the onion, leaving the root on, then halve it from top to root and peel. Lay each half flat-side down and slice across as thinly as you can. Put the slices in a small bowl with {1 tbsp} vinegar, {1 tsp} honey and a pinch of salt, stir and leave for at least 15–20 minutes.
2. **Parsley, if fresh.** Bunch the leaves tight on the plate and slice through a few times. Set aside.
3. **Honey butter.** In a small bowl, mash {6 tbsp} softened butter with a fork, add {3 tbsp} honey a spoonful at a time and keep mashing until smooth. Stir in the parsley.
4. **Spice mix.** In another small bowl, stir together {1½ tsp} salt, {1 tsp} black pepper, {2 tsp} paprika and {2 tsp} garlic powder. Share it evenly between the batches of chicken.
5. **Season the chicken on the plate in batches.** For faster cooking, first cut each thigh into 3–4 strips on the plate (you slice it later anyway). Lay 3–4 thighs' worth on the plate, sprinkle with some of the spice mix and a little of the {1 tbsp} oil, rub in, flip and repeat, then put them straight into the air fryer basket. Take the spice mix with a clean spoon so raw chicken never touches the bowl. Or skip the plate: tip the chicken, spice mix and oil into a food bag, seal and squish for a minute. The bag can sit in the fridge overnight, so on prep day it goes straight into the air fryer.
6. **Air fry** at 200°C (preheated for 3 minutes) until 75°C in the thickest part, or white all the way through with clear juices. **Whole thighs:** 12–18 minutes, 3–4 at a time in a single layer, turned halfway (if they overlap, add 3–5 minutes and swap top and bottom halfway). **Strips:** 10–12 minutes, shaking the basket halfway; a loose pile is fine, so about twice as much fits per batch. While the first batch cooks, make the onions and honey butter (steps 1–3) if you haven't already.
7. **Wash the plate** in hot soapy water. Rest the cooked chicken on it for 5 minutes, then slice. Let it cool completely before assembling.
8. **Assemble:** spread honey butter on the inside of both slices of bread, then add the provolone and sliced chicken. Don't toast yet.
9. **Wrap** each sandwich tightly in foil or cling film. Keep the onions in their own tub.
10. **On the day:** unwrap and air fry at 180°C for 6–8 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp, melted and hot through (a minute or two longer straight from the fridge). Put something light and oven-safe on top so the top slice doesn't blow off. Open up, add the onions and any spinach or rocket, and eat with the veg.

### Kit
- 1 sharp knife and a steel plate, with a damp tea towel or kitchen roll underneath so it doesn't slide.
- 3 small bowls: onions, honey butter, spice mix.
- 1 fork (honey butter) and 1 spoon (spice mix).
- Food bags (optional), to season all the chicken at once instead of on the plate.
- Foil or cling film and a small tub for the onions.
- Meat thermometer (optional), to check 75°C.

### Swaps
- **Lazy day, no honey butter to make:** spread the bread with plain butter or light spread and drizzle honey straight over the chicken. Same flavour, no bowl.
- **Lazy day, even quicker:** a drizzle of shop-bought hot honey, honey mustard or BBQ sauce over the chicken, or a squeeze of sriracha with a drizzle of honey.
- **Instead of the butter, mix the honey with:** light mayo (honey mayo; spreads easily and toasts golden), light cream cheese (thicker and creamy), or 0% Greek yoghurt (lowest calorie and adds protein, but tangy). Use the same amount as the butter.
- Spread yoghurt or cream cheese on the inside of the bread only; for toasting, the outside needs butter, spread or mayo to crisp.

### Healthier
- **Use 1 slice of provolone per sandwich** instead of 2, and spread about 1 tbsp of honey butter per sandwich (half the honey in the mix). That saves about 85 kcal and 7g of free sugars per sandwich.
- Light spread is already in the recipe; it's lower in fat than butter.
- Pile spinach or rocket inside and have veg on the side to fill up.
- 1½ tsp salt in the spice mix is a lot: 1 tsp cuts the salt noticeably.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- The plate does everything, so work in this order: onions and parsley, then raw chicken, then wash it, then cooked chicken. Raw chicken never shares the plate with anything eaten raw.
- Thighs are forgiving; a few extra minutes won't dry them out the way breast does.
- Pickle the whole onion if you like, with double the vinegar and honey; it keeps 5 days and goes in other meals too.
- About 2 thighs (180–225g raw chicken) per sandwich is the same chicken as a rice-and-two-thighs lunch. The page shows how many sandwiches your grams make and how much chicken goes in each.
- Prep time is about 1 hour for 4 sandwiches with whole thighs, mostly air fryer batches. Strips and chicken seasoned the night before bring it to 35–40 minutes, and they matter most for bigger packs: 1.5kg is about 5 batches whole, or about 3 as strips.
- Hands-on it's about 20–25 minutes more than a rice-and-thighs prep (onions, honey butter, assembling and wrapping), and each day takes 6–8 minutes to toast.

### Storage
- **Fridge:** wrapped, untoasted sandwiches keep 3–4 days (UK guidance for cooked chicken is 2 days, so freeze the later ones if you'd rather be safe).
- **Freezer:** freeze wrapped sandwiches for up to 2 months. Put the wrapped sandwiches in a freezer bag, squeeze the air out and write the date on it so the bread doesn't dry out. Don't refreeze.
- **Weekly plan:** keep 2 in the fridge for the first 2 days and freeze the rest. Each night, move one from the freezer to the fridge; it's thawed by lunch, then toast as in step 10.
- **Don't toast before storing:** a toasted sandwich goes soggy in the fridge and dries out when toasted again.
- Keep the pickled onions in a sealed tub in the fridge for up to 5 days, and add them after toasting so the bread stays crisp.
- Honey butter keeps about 2 weeks in the fridge; let it soften before spreading.

## 9. Smash burger tacos
*Thin beef mince pressed onto a tortilla and air fried until browned and crisp, with cheese, burger sauce and lettuce. About 365 calories and 29g protein each.*

**Makes:** 4 tacos

**Lighter:** cheese slices → light cheese slices · burger sauce ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 6–8 min, then 1 min with cheese · beef-side up, no flipping

### Tacos
- 450g (1 lb) lean beef mince, 5% fat (or chicken mince)
- 4 small flour tortillas
- 4 cheese slices (light slices for fewer calories)
- 4 tbsp burger sauce (shop-bought, or homemade, see Swaps)
- Shredded lettuce
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Method
1. **Press the mince on thin.** Divide the mince between the tortillas (about 110g each) and spread it right to the edges with your fingers or the back of a spoon, about as thick as a £1 coin. Thin is what makes it brown and cook through. Season with salt and pepper.
2. **Air fry beef-side up** at 200°C for 6–8 minutes, 1–2 at a time depending on the basket, until the mince is browned with no pink left (75°C in the middle, or cooked all the way through with clear juices). Don't flip: the heat from the top browns the beef while the tortilla crisps underneath. No preheat needed; the beef weighs the tortilla down so it doesn't blow around.
3. **Melt the cheese:** lay a slice on each and air fry for 1 more minute.
4. **Finish:** add 1 tbsp burger sauce and a handful of lettuce to each, fold and eat straight away.

### Kit
- A plate or board to press the mince onto the tortillas, and a spoon to spread it.
- Tongs or a spatula to lift the tacos out of the basket.

### Swaps
- **Homemade burger sauce (about 4 tbsp):** 2 tbsp light mayo, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tsp mustard and a chopped gherkin.
- **Chicken mince:** same method, cooked to 75°C. It's leaner and paler, so add ½ tsp smoked paprika with the salt and pepper for colour.

### Healthier
- **Use light cheese slices** and half the burger sauce (½ tbsp per taco). That's about 50 kcal less per taco.
- Make the burger sauce with light mayo (see Swaps).
- Have 1 taco with a big portion of veg instead of 2 tacos.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- **As a snack:** 1 taco, about 365 calories and 29g protein. It's a big snack, so it fits best after training or on a busy day.
- **As a main:** 2 tacos with a big handful of salad or a portion of frozen veg, about 730 calories and 58g protein. Three is too much for a regular meal unless it's a big training day.
- If the tortilla edges brown too fast, drop to 190°C and add a minute.
- You won't get the smashed crust a pan gives on the beef; the air fryer browns the top instead and makes the tortilla crunchier.
- 92/8 mince in the original is about the same as UK 5% fat steak mince. A 500g pack makes 4–5 tacos.

### Storage
- **Best fresh:** the tortilla goes soft once the beef is cooked on it, so make these to order. They take about 10 minutes.
- **Raw mince:** keep in the fridge and use by its date, or freeze in 110g portions for up to 3 months and thaw overnight in the fridge.
- **Leftover cooked tacos:** fridge within 2 hours and eat within 1–2 days. Reheat at 180°C for 3–4 minutes until piping hot, then add the sauce and lettuce. Reheat only once.

## 10. Honey glazed chicken burgers
*Thin breast fillets in a crushed cornflake crust, air fried and brushed with honey glaze, in a toasted bun with burger sauce.*

**Makes:** 4 burgers

**Lighter:** honey ×½ · butter ×0 · mayonnaise → 0% fat Greek yoghurt · Greek yoghurt → 0% fat Greek yoghurt · sweet chilli sauce ×0

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 12–15 min · spray with oil, turn and spray halfway

### Chicken
- 680g chicken breasts (about 4), 1 per burger
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

### Breading
- 100g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 200g cornflakes, crushed
- Cooking oil spray

### Honey glaze
- 4 tbsp honey
- 1½ tbsp butter
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp sriracha (optional)

### Burger sauce
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tsp lemon juice (or lime)

### To assemble
- 4 burger buns
- Lettuce

### Method
1. **Slice the breasts.** Lay each breast flat on the plate (damp towel underneath), put your hand flat on top and slice through sideways into 2 thin fillets (2 fillets = 1 breast = 1 burger). If one end is much thicker, put it in a food bag and press it flat with your fist or the bottom of a mug so it cooks evenly. **Whole breast instead:** skip the slicing; put each breast in a food bag and press or bash the thick end until it's an even 2cm all over.
2. **Season** the fillets on both sides with {2 tsp} paprika, {2 tsp} garlic powder, {1 tsp} onion powder, {1 tsp} salt and {1 tsp} black pepper.
3. **Crush the cornflakes** in a food bag with your fist or a mug until they're small crumbs with a few bigger flakes.
4. **Bread:** coat each fillet in flour, then beaten egg, then cornflakes, pressing the crumbs on firmly. Use one small bowl each for the flour, egg and cornflakes, or shake the fillets in the flour in a food bag to save a bowl. Rest for 10 minutes so the coating sets.
5. **Air fry** at 200°C in a single layer, sprayed with oil, turning and spraying halfway, until golden and 75°C in the thickest part (white all the way through): 12–15 minutes for thin fillets, 15–18 minutes for a flattened whole breast (drop to 180°C for the last few minutes if the crust browns before the middle is done). Cook in batches so the pieces don't overlap.
6. **Glaze:** in a small bowl, microwave {4 tbsp} honey and {1½ tbsp} butter for about 20 seconds until melted, then stir in {1 tsp} paprika, {½ tsp} garlic powder and the sriracha. No brush needed: spoon a little over each hot fillet and spread it with the back of the spoon, then flip and repeat. Or drop each fillet into the bowl, turn it once with a fork and lift it out.
7. **Burger sauce:** mix {4 tbsp} mayonnaise, {2 tbsp} Greek yoghurt, {1 tbsp} sweet chilli sauce and {1 tsp} lemon juice until smooth.
8. **Assemble:** toast the buns in the air fryer at 180°C for 1–2 minutes, spread with burger sauce, add lettuce and both glazed fillets. Eat straight away.

### Kit
- 1 sharp knife and a plate, with a damp tea towel or kitchen roll underneath so it doesn't slide.
- 3 small bowls: flour, egg and cornflakes for breading; wash one for the glaze.
- 2 food bags: one to crush the cornflakes, one to flatten thick fillets or shake them in flour.
- A fork to beat the egg and mix the sauce, and a spoon to spread the glaze (no brush needed).
- Meat thermometer (optional), to check 75°C.

### Swaps
- **Lazy glaze:** drizzle honey straight over the hot chicken with a squeeze of sriracha, or use shop-bought hot honey, sweet chilli sauce or BBQ sauce.
- **Glaze without butter:** warm the honey on its own with 1 tsp hot water so it brushes on easily, or use light spread or 1 tsp oil.
- **Lazy burger sauce:** shop-bought burger sauce, light mayo with a squeeze of sweet chilli, or mayo mixed with ketchup.
- **Lighter burger sauce:** use light mayo, or skip the mayo and use 6 tbsp 0% Greek yoghurt (lowest calorie and adds protein).
- **No sweet chilli:** ½ tbsp honey and a few drops of sriracha.
- **No lemon or lime:** a splash of vinegar.
- **No slicing:** use mini fillets (tenderloins) instead of breasts, 3 per burger.

### Patty options
- **Crispy cornflake chicken** (this recipe): the crunchiest, but the coating and glaze add carbs and sugar.
- **Peri-peri chicken** (recipe 11): a flattened breast in your homemade peri-peri blend, no breading or glaze. Much less sugar and carbs.
- **Beef** (recipe 12): 5% fat steak mince patties with cheese and burger sauce.
- **Plain seasoned breast:** skip the breading and glaze; rub the flattened breast with the spice mix and a little oil and air fry at 200°C for 12–16 minutes to 75°C. The lowest-calorie option.
- **Any blend from Part 1:** use about 2 tbsp blend and 1 tbsp oil per 600g breast (see How to use the blends), then cook as for plain seasoned breast. Cajun, smoky all-rounder and lemon & herb all work.
- **Chicken mince patties:** make recipe 12 with chicken mince instead of beef, cooked to 75°C.

### Healthier
- **The glaze is the main sugar:** 4 tbsp honey is about 17g of sugar per burger, and with the bun, cornflakes and sweet chilli one burger has about 27g of free sugars, close to the NHS limit of 30g a day.
- **Halve the honey** to {2 tbsp} and loosen it with 1 tsp hot water instead of butter. It still coats every fillet, saves about 9g of sugar and 65 kcal per burger, and cuts the saturated fat.
- **Glaze one side only,** or drizzle a little honey with a squeeze of sriracha instead of making the glaze.
- **Burger sauce:** use 0% Greek yoghurt instead of mayonnaise (about 90 kcal less per burger and a little more protein), and sriracha instead of sweet chilli.
- **Bun:** a wholemeal or seeded bun adds fibre; a thin bun or a lettuce wrap cuts carbs.
- **Fill up on veg:** extra lettuce, tomato and cucumber in the bun, or veg on the side.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with the honey halved, no butter, yoghurt sauce and no sweet chilli.

### Tips
- **Fillets or whole breast:** 2 thin fillets cook faster and give more crunchy coating per bite; a flattened whole breast skips the slicing but takes a few minutes longer. Same chicken and same nutrition either way.
- One breast (about 170g) per burger is about the same chicken as 2 thighs or 3 tenderloins. The original recipe uses half a breast per burger, which is small.
- Press the cornflakes on firmly and let the coating rest; it stays on better when you turn the fillets.
- Keep one hand for the flour and cornflakes and the other for the egg, so your fingers don't get clumpy.
- Glaze just before eating; the honey softens the crust if it sits.

### Storage
- **Best fresh:** the crust is crispiest straight from the air fryer.
- **Don't store assembled burgers:** the bun goes soggy and the crust softens. Keep the 5 parts in separate containers and build each burger on the day:
- **1. Cooked chicken (fridge or freezer):** cool completely and leave unglazed. Put each burger's 2 fillets (or 1 whole breast) together, with kitchen paper or baking paper between pieces so they don't stick. Fridge in an airtight box within 2 hours and eat within 2 days, or wrap each portion in foil, bag, date and freeze for up to 3 months.
- **2. Glaze (fridge):** in a covered bowl or jar for up to 1 week. Microwave for 10 seconds to loosen before using.
- **3. Burger sauce (fridge):** in a small sealed tub for up to 3 days; don't freeze it.
- **4. Buns (cupboard or freezer):** in their own bag at room temperature, or freeze them and toast from frozen for 2–3 minutes.
- **5. Lettuce (fridge):** washed, dried and in a box lined with kitchen paper so it stays crisp.
- **Weekly plan:** keep 2 burgers' worth of chicken in the fridge and freeze the rest. Each night, move one portion from the freezer to the fridge.
- **On the day:** reheat the chicken at 180°C for 5–6 minutes (8–10 from frozen) until piping hot, spoon on the glaze, toast the bun for 1–2 minutes, then spread with sauce and add lettuce and chicken. Reheat only once.
- **Freeze it raw instead:** freeze breaded, uncooked fillets flat on a tray, then bag them. Cook from frozen at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, turning halfway, until 75°C in the thickest part. This gives the crispiest result for later in the week.

## 11. Peri-peri chicken burgers
*Flattened chicken breast marinated in the homemade peri-peri blend, air fried and served with peri-peri mayo, lettuce and tomato. No breading, no glaze.*

**Lighter:** light mayo → 0% fat Greek yoghurt

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 12–16 min · turn halfway

### Chicken
- 680g chicken breasts (about 4), 1 per burger
- 2¼ tbsp peri-peri blend (see Peri-peri in Part 1)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 lemon, zest and juice (keep a wedge back to squeeze over)
- 1 tbsp vinegar (optional)

### Peri-peri mayo
- 4 tbsp light mayo
- 1 tsp peri-peri blend
- 1 tsp lemon juice

### Peri-peri yoghurt sauce (optional, lighter instead of the mayo)
- 4 tbsp 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 1½ tsp peri-peri blend
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Pinch of garlic powder

### To assemble
- 4 burger buns
- 1 tomato, sliced
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- Lettuce

### Method
1. **Flatten the breasts.** Put each breast in a food bag and press or bash the thick end with your fist or the bottom of a mug until it's an even 2cm all over. Or slice each one sideways into 2 thin fillets on the plate (damp towel underneath).
2. **Marinate.** In the same food bag (or a small bowl), mix the chicken with {1 tbsp} oil, {2¼ tbsp} peri-peri blend, the lemon zest and juice and the vinegar if using. Squish to coat and leave at least 15 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
3. **Make one sauce.** **Peri-peri mayo:** in a small bowl, mix {4 tbsp} light mayo, {1 tsp} peri-peri blend and {1 tsp} lemon juice. **Or peri-peri yoghurt sauce (lighter):** mix {4 tbsp} 0% fat Greek yoghurt, {1½ tsp} peri-peri blend, {1 tsp} lemon juice and a pinch of garlic powder, then leave for 5 minutes so the spices soften. - **Thighs instead of breast:** 2 boneless thighs per burger, air fried for 15–18 minutes.
- **Add cheese:** a light cheese slice in the last minute of cooking.

### Healthier
- This is the lightest chicken burger here: no breading and no glaze, so the sugar is mostly the 2 tsp in the blend and the bun.
- **Make the peri-peri yoghurt sauce instead of the mayo** (its own ingredient list above, step 3). It has more protein and fewer calories; for a creamier sauce, go half yoghurt, half light mayo.
- **Bun:** a wholemeal or seeded bun adds fibre, or wrap in big lettuce leaves to cut carbs.
- Pile on extra salad, or have veg on the side.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with the yoghurt sauce.

### Tips
- The fresh squeeze of lemon after cooking is what makes it taste like peri-peri; the heat dulls the marinade.
- The sauce is about 1 tbsp per burger, a normal spread on one half of the bun. If you like it saucy, make 1½ times as much.
- Marinating overnight in the bag makes prep day quicker and the chicken tastier.
- One breast (about 170g) per burger is about the same chicken as 2 thighs or 3 tenderloins.

### Storage
- **Store unassembled,** in separate containers: cooked chicken, peri-peri mayo, buns, and lettuce, tomato and onion. Build each burger on the day.
- **Cooked chicken:** cool, then fridge in an airtight box within 2 hours and eat within 2 days, or wrap each portion in foil, bag, date and freeze for up to 3 months. Reheat at 180°C for 5–6 minutes (8–10 from frozen) until piping hot. Reheat only once.

- **Peri-peri mayo or yoghurt sauce:** sealed tub in the fridge for up to 3 days; don't freeze it.
- **Buns:** in their bag at room temperature, or frozen and toasted from frozen for 2–3 minutes.
- **Salad:** lettuce in a box lined with kitchen paper; slice tomato and onion on the day if you can.

## 12. Beef burgers
*Lean beef patties air fried with melted cheese, burger sauce, lettuce, tomato and onion.*

**Makes:** 4 burgers

**Lighter:** cheese slices → light cheese slices · light mayo ×½

**Air fryer:** 200°C · 8–10 min, then 1 min with cheese · flip halfway

### Patties
- 500g lean beef mince, 5% fat (or chicken mince)
- ¾ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder

### Burger sauce
- 2 tbsp light mayo
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 gherkin, finely chopped

### To assemble
- 4 burger buns
- 4 cheese slices
- 1 tomato, sliced
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- Lettuce

### Method
1. **Veg first, on the clean plate:** slice the onion and tomato and finely chop the gherkin, then set aside.
2. **Burger sauce:** in a small bowl, mix {2 tbsp} light mayo, {1 tbsp} ketchup, {1 tsp} mustard and the chopped gherkin.
3. **Shape the patties.** Divide the mince into 4 (about 125g each), roll into balls and press each one flat on the plate, about 1.5cm thick and a little wider than the bun (they shrink). Press a dimple in the middle with your thumb so they stay flat. Handle the meat as little as you can so the burgers stay tender.
4. **Season** both sides with {¾ tsp} salt, {½ tsp} black pepper, {1 tsp} garlic powder and {1 tsp} onion powder, mixed together first.
5. **Air fry** at 200°C for 8–10 minutes, 2 at a time, flipping halfway, until cooked through with no pink in the middle (75°C). Mince must always be cooked all the way through.
6. **Melt the cheese:** lay a slice on each patty and air fry for 1 more minute.
7. **Assemble:** toast the buns at 180°C for 1–2 minutes, spread with burger sauce, then add lettuce, the cheesy patty, tomato and onion.

### Kit
- A plate to shape the patties on (wash it before cooked food goes on it), and a sharp knife for the veg.
- 1 small bowl and a spoon for the sauce, and a small bowl or cup to mix the seasoning.
- A spatula or tongs to flip the patties.
- Meat thermometer (optional), to check 75°C.

### Swaps
- **Lazy sauce:** shop-bought burger sauce, or ketchup and mustard straight on the bun.
- **Chicken mince:** same method, cooked to 75°C; add ½ tsp smoked paprika for colour.
- **Smash-style:** make 8 thinner patties (about 60g each) and stack 2 per burger; cook for 6–7 minutes.
- **No gherkin:** a splash of vinegar in the sauce gives the same tang.

### Healthier
- 5% fat mince is already lean; it's the cheese and sauce that add most of the fat.
- **Use light cheese slices** and half the burger sauce. Mustard and ketchup on their own are lower in fat.
- **Bun:** a wholemeal or seeded bun adds fibre, or wrap in big lettuce leaves to cut carbs.
- Pile on extra salad, or have veg on the side.
- Tap **Lighter** on the nutrition card to see the numbers with these swaps.

### Tips
- Make the patties wider than the bun; beef shrinks as it cooks.
- The thumb dimple stops the patties puffing up into balls.
- Salt only the outside, just before cooking; salt mixed in makes the texture sausage-like.

### Storage
- **Store unassembled,** in separate containers: patties, burger sauce, buns, and lettuce, tomato and onion. Build each burger on the day.
- **Raw patties:** shape them, layer with baking paper, and fridge for up to 1 day or freeze flat for up to 3 months. Cook from frozen at 200°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway, until 75°C in the middle.
- **Cooked patties:** cool, then fridge in an airtight box within 2 hours and eat within 2 days, or freeze for up to 3 months. Reheat at 180°C for 4–5 minutes (8–10 from frozen) until piping hot, then add the cheese for the last minute. Reheat only once.
- **Burger sauce:** sealed tub in the fridge for up to 3 days; don't freeze it.
- **Buns:** in their bag at room temperature, or frozen and toasted from frozen for 2–3 minutes.
